WHY WE EXIST

People, not assumptions.

Every person has value. No one should be reduced to a label, a difficult experience, or someone else's expectations.

The Hidden Gem Project aims to make mental health information more accessible, encourage individualized support, and build a culture where asking for help is met with understanding instead of stigma.

We believe people deserve to be understood as individuals. Our focus is spreading awareness, sharing helpful resources, and connecting with communities that share this goal.

The Hidden Gem Project provides awareness and educational information. This website does not diagnose conditions or provide therapy.

Our Founding Story

The Hidden Gem Project was founded by Caleb Moistner, who originated the idea and vision behind the project. His goal is to promote mental health awareness, encourage individualized support, and build a culture where every person feels valued and free from judgment.

Hunter Michael contributed to the development of the project's theme and motto, helping shape how its message and mission are presented.
